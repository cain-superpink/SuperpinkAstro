I didn’t go into this one with the intention of creating a horrorscape, however throughout the project it certainly developed that way. I furthered my exploration of evolving textures, this time with a combination of recorded and synthesized sounds. Sounds are stretched out, looped and chopped up to walk the line between familiarity and the unknown. Towards the end of the work a semblance of melody can be found. Once again, headphones should be used for an optimal experience.
