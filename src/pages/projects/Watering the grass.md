An ambisonic piece that isn’t creepy, this is a pure soundscape using only field recorded sounds. A peaceful stroll through the park featuring running water, rustling leaves, chirping birds and maybe some distant signs of human life. The focus of the work is predominantly the manipulation of the frequency spectrum and how it informs the texture of the sounds.
