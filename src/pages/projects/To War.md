A narrative contiunition of the previous track. ‘To War’ loses the melodic elements of a call to arms as the sounds of battle take the stage. All sounds in this track are synthesized, this was an excercise in modular synthesis. Much of my aim was to create a synthesized replication of real sounds, which is evident at the beginning of the piece as the synthesizer emulates a train leaving the station while horses gallop to battle. These sounds exist throughout the piece but as the chaos erupts throughout the track, the sounds become more metaphorical and rather than display the sounds of battle, I instead tried to represent how it might feel.
