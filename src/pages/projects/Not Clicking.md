‘Not Clicking’ is the first of three ambisonic works. Ambisonics allow the placement of sound in a 3D sphere, allowing the listener to hear height and depth moreso than standard stereo audio. For the full experience these pieces should be listened to with headphones as they use head-related transfer function (HRTF) to emulate a 3D soundscape. The video uses youtubes 360 video feature so the video controls can be used to rotate around the space. This paricular piece is inspired by horror films and utilizes an extended dynamic range. I used many processing techniques to explore the timbral possibilities of field recorded sounds.
