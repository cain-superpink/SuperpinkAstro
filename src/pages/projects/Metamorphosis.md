A 30 minute video essay analysing the music and story of Weezer’s 1996 Album ‘Pinkerton’. This was an entirely solo project which meant it was good practice for writing, recording and editing. I learned a lot about animation and motion graphics during the process, making sure to include a musical flair by scoring much of the video myself, taking inspiration from the harmonic structure of Pinkerton’s sound.
