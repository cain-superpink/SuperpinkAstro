It’s difficult to define the genre of this track, it’s a lot of noise and a lot of fuzz. ‘Electronic Post-Punk’ is what I’ll go with. This was one of my first attempts at singing on a track, and it was the first where I was happy with the results, particularly in the bridge where the noisy buildup gives way to punk euphoria
